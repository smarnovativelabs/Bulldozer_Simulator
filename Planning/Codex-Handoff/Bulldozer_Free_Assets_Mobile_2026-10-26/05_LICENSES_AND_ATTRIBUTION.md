# Licenses, edits and credits

Checked 26 October 2026. Save the exact license bundled with each downloaded version, creator/source URL, acquisition date and your modifications. These notes summarize the selected sources; they cannot guarantee that a game will never receive a claim.

| License | Practical handling |
|---|---|
| [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Allows reuse and modification, including commercial use, without an attribution condition. Keep provenance anyway. Selected Kenney and Quaternius packs use this. |
| [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Commercial adaptation is allowed with appropriate creator credit, source/license links and indication of changes. Selected Sketchfab models use this. Recoloring or reshaping does not remove those obligations. |
| [Standard Unity Asset Store EULA](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms) | Free does not mean public-domain. Non-restricted assets can be modified and embedded in a qualifying original product under its conditions. Do not redistribute raw assets as a public asset pack; retain notices and check applicable seat/entity terms. |
| [Unity Companion License](https://unity.com/legal/licenses/unity-companion-license) | The selected official controller uses this separate license. Use is tied to authoring/distributing content under a valid Unity Engine license; preserve license/copyright and third-party notices. |

For an AI-assisted workflow, note that the current Asset Store EULA §2.2.1.1(g) includes restrictions on AI/ML uses and asset inputs. Do not assume vendor files can be supplied to an AI model merely because they are free or stored locally. Prefer the selected CC0 sources for asset experiments; verify permission before using EULA-covered files as model inputs. Ordinary Blender editing for the game and AI processing are distinct uses. This research pack contains our notes and links, not vendor asset files. [Current terms](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms).

License labels do not grant unrelated trademark or personality rights. Use original branding and avoid recognisable real manufacturers' logos. The reference game's extracted APK assets, audio, UI and characters are not an asset source for this project. A small Blender change is not a substitute for having a license.
