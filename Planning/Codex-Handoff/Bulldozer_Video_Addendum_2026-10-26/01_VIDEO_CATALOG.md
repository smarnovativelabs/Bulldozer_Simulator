## Evidence handling

Ordinary web retrieval failed for the supplied YouTube links, so the pages and video frames were inspected through the browser. Intro logos, YouTube ads and stale frames during buffering were excluded. No complete audio transcript was obtained. Small text in low-resolution portrait footage was omitted where unreliable.

There are no newly saved screenshot files in this addendum. The original pack contains [12 earlier reference captures](../bulldozer-master-research/07_SOURCES_AND_EVIDENCE.md). Every new observation has a video ID, timestamp, link and limitation in [video_evidence.json](video_evidence.json).

The APK review describes version 11.3.0. These recordings do not display a verified build version, so their UI and tuning are not assumed to match that APK or one another. Relative upload labels were not converted into guessed publication dates.
