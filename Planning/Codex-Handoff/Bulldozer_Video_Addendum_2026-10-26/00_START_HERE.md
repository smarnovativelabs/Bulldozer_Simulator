# Bulldozer video research for the Unity prototype

Updated 26 October 2026. The reference keeps a consistent mining, carrying, processing and selling loop while expanding machinery, cargo size and site themes. For our original game, the most useful immediate lessons are readable cargo, clear destinations, satisfying controlled break effects and dependable delivery accounting.

## Coverage

The supplied [DARIO_111 playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLeWEdw2WOkRc) is valid and lists **17 episodes** on this date. All 17 are indexed. Episodes 2–17 received new visual samples; episode 1 uses six earlier samples. All four supplied standalone videos are included, with 36 newly inspected paused frames across 20 videos in total. These are selected checkpoints, not full watch-throughs or transcripts. The source register covers 21 distinct videos when the earlier episode 1 review is included.

This corrects the earlier pack's statement that the playlist was incomplete or unverified. The earlier files remain intact as historical research.
