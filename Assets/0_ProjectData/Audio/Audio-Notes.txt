Quarry audio

Quarry_Dawn_Underscore.wav: original 48-second instrumental composition generated for this project from synthesized tones, 80 BPM, no sampled/reference-game music. Source recipe: Maintenance/Audio/create_audio.py.
Stone_Break, Ore_Pickup, Ore_Place, Refinery_Ready, Delivery_Confirm, Refinery_Loop and Truck_Engine_Loop: original synthesized effects from the same recipe.
impactMining_000/001/002, footstep_concrete_000/001/002 and impactMetal_light_001: copies from the project's Kenney Impact Sounds pack. CC0 license included in Kenney-License.txt.

Runtime controller: QuarryAudio on persistent CommonUI prefab. Music volume 0.12 before master Sound Level; pause/loading reduces it to 45% of that. Master Sound and Sound Level affect all music, gameplay and Feel UI sounds.
Fixed budget: 8 reusable world effect voices, 3 reusable machine loops and 1 streaming music source. Existing cached Feel UI sources remain in use. No runtime audio objects are created per hit, pickup or delivery.
All spatial effects are mono at 24 kHz. Music is stereo Vorbis streaming; short effects and four-second machine loops are preloaded for low runtime decode cost. No AudioMixer asset or plugin audio assets were changed.
