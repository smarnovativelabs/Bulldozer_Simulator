Environment:
- 12 natural boulders in the first three rows of the west ore bed. Four shared meshes, 168 triangles each, existing shared stone atlas material, no per-rock material instances.

- Ground and mineral colors toned down for a less saturated quarry palette.
- The remaining 66 rocks retain their existing forms; this is the requested small pilot section, not a complete quarry art replacement.

Round loop:
- HUD displays excavation number and rocks remaining.
- Completion requires every registered rock depleted AND all loose ore, backpack/cart inventory, refinery queues, loading pad and truck inventory settled.
- Existing last-partial-truck dispatch remains supported.
- Bonus is $50 for excavation 1, +$10 per following excavation, capped at $150.
- Bonus cash and claimed flag commit together. Reopening the app cannot award it twice.
- Next Excavation commits the next index and cleared depletion state before fading/reloading the SAME scene. Double clicks cannot skip rounds.
- Cash, damage/capacity/speed upgrades, delivery milestones and cart ownership remain. The empty cart returns parked at its dock and the player starts on foot.
- Fixed safe spawn positions are reused. Later rounds vary bounded rock durability; natural boulders vary their visual yaw. No unlimited health scaling or runtime geometry generation.
- Version 3 saves migrate to version 4 without losing progress. Future versions remain protected from overwrite.

- Feel MMF_Player controls the existing pooled break dust and completion title feedback; initialized explicitly. Two players are reused, no per-hit feedback object instantiation.
- Existing sparkle, chip and fracture pools retained. No rigidbody debris added.
