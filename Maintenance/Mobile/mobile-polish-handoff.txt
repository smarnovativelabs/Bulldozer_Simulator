Mobile usability and performance polish - 29 October 2026

Changes (no new gameplay features or build):
1. Volume slider now has a 56-unit transparent touch surface while its visible 12-unit bar remains unchanged. Sound/vibration toggle raycast bounds expand to 56 units high without stretching artwork. Edge-hit regressions that failed before now pass at every requested resolution.
2. QuarryGameUI no longer copies snapshots, concatenates arrays or refreshes inventory/upgrade/settings text while those panels are hidden. Cash updates only when its value changes. Open inventory uses a reusable 12-element counting array; all four storage tabs retain correct totals.
3. Disabled shadow casting only on tiny Embedded Minerals meshes on rocks. Rock geometry, textures, colliders and main rock shadows remain unchanged. Repeated frozen-world, same-camera Editor A/B: 210 batches / 58,575 triangles with patch shadows versus 176 batches / 50,871 triangles without; SetPass stays 15. Both repeated measurements matched. This is about 16% fewer batches in that view, not a device FPS claim.

Fresh-player session:
360.1 seconds of normal running Play mode with a fresh isolated save. Automated NavMesh-guided joystick walking, with no teleporting between rocks/stations. Actual pickaxe contacts, pickup detection, production timers, truck deliveries and workbench purchases ran normally.
Outcome: 26 rocks, 61 successful contacts, 78 refined items, 22 deliveries, cash $1935. No route/station deadlock. First pickaxe upgrade bought at 8.6 seconds, first delivery at 20.9 seconds. These are timings for an automated driver that immediately knows the controls/routes, not measured human onboarding or proof of fun/retention.
Busy-session Editor maxima sampled: 335 batches, 27 SetPass, 63 active particles, 7 playing AudioSources. The high whole-session batch peak was investigated with the controlled shadow A/B above. Whole-Editor allocated memory peaked near 1.35 GB; this includes Editor/package overhead and is not player/device memory.
