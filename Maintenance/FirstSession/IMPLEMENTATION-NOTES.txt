FIRST-SESSION GAMEPLAY UPDATE - 2026-11-03

Behavior
- Fresh saves use 12 starter rocks (36 ore): 3 rocks at 2 HP, 5 at 4 HP, 4 at 6 HP. The next excavation restores the normal 78-rock quarry; Copper Ridge still opens after excavation 3 with 84 rocks.
- Existing first pickaxe upgrade costs $10 and changes damage from 1 to 2. Starter rock hits therefore change from 2/4/6 to 1/2/3. The existing two-rock bonus funds it; purchase is optional.
- Completing the shorter first excavation permanently grants the wheelbarrow. Previously owned carts are not charged again.
- A full bag stops mining only until the first successful raw-ore refinery deposit. The permission is persisted atomically with that deposit. Pickup capacity remains enforced. Subsequent full bags do not stop swings, animation contacts or rock destruction; extra ore remains saved on the ground.
- Cinemachine shows the refinery on the first full bag, the optional workbench after first deposit when the starter upgrade is affordable, and the cart after ownership unlocks. Each location is shown once per save. Each look blends out and back, can be skipped by tap/click/Space/Escape/gamepad South, and restores camera yaw and controls. Pause, disable and scene transitions release only the guidance input lock.
- Camera hints show complete text immediately. Workbench/cart camera angles stay below the shed roof. Existing arrow guidance continues afterward; buying an upgrade is not required.

Save safety
- Schema 9 adds shortFirstShift, firstRefineryDeposit and guidanceSeenMask.
- Versions 1-8 retain their original excavation layout and migrate to completed onboarding, without resetting balances, upgrades, cargo, partial rock damage or permanent ownership.
- All runtime tests use the SessionState override Bulldozer.Mining.TestSavePath under Temp. Normal progress is not reset. A fresh install/save is needed to see the new first-shift flow; the existing personal test save intentionally retains its old progression.

Performance (Editor counts, not device FPS)
- Ore remains pooled with six shared materials and up to six GPU-instanced ore submissions on supported devices. First shift prewarms 36 slots; normal quarry 234; Copper Ridge 252. The safety cap is 256, sufficient for all 84 rocks without pickup. Future larger areas must revise this budget.
- Largest ore mesh is 44 triangles, so 252 visible ore are at most 11,088 ore triangles. Non-instancing device fallback still needs device profiling.
- Guidance adds two non-rendering virtual cameras, no meshes/materials/particles/lights, and reuses the gameplay rendering camera and existing UI.
- Both delivery truck skinned bodies remain 4,400 triangles, one renderer/material and six wheel transforms; rigs unchanged.
